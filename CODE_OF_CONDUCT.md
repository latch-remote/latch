# Code of conduct

Latch follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The short form:

## Our pledge

Everyone taking part in this project — in issues, pull requests,
discussions and anywhere else the project meets — can expect a
harassment-free experience, whatever their age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good faith.
- Criticise code and ideas, not people.
- Accept that others weigh trade-offs differently, and say why you
  disagree.
- Own mistakes, and fix them.

## Not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome advances of any kind.
- Insults, personal or political attacks, trolling.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit
  permission.

## Enforcement

The maintainers decide what is appropriate here and may edit, hide or
remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions, and may ban
people temporarily or permanently, for behaviour they find inappropriate,
threatening, offensive or harmful.

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through the contact options
on [@MrBeldum's GitHub profile](https://github.com/MrBeldum), or use
GitHub's own *Report content* on the comment in question. Reports are
handled confidentially, and reporters are not named.
